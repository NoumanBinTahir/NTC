NTC's Profile

Establishment of NTC

NTC has been Established under Pakistan Telecom (Re-Org) Act 1996 to provide secure and reliable telecom services to Federal/Provincial Government Departments and Institutions as the Federal Government may determine. For this purpose, NTC focuses to build, operate and maintain telecommunication systems throughout Pakistan to fulfill the telecom needs of Government departments.

Mission

To provide secure and reliable Telecommunication Services to Government Departments / Organizations all over Pakistan less Azad Jamu & Kashmir and Northern Areas

Mandate

To provide telecommunication and data services to Armed Forces, Defense Projects, Federal Government, Provincial Governments or such other Governmental Agencies or Governmental Institutions as The Federal Government may determine from time to time.

Progress Since Inception

It is constitutional responsibility of Federal Government of Pakistan to provide Telecom/ICT services to all its departments. On behalf of Federal Government of, this constitutional function is being performed by National Telecommunication Corporation. NTC has been established under Pakistan Telecom (Re-Org) Act 1996 to provide secure and reliable telecom services to Federal/Provincial Government Departments and Institutions as the Federal Government may determine. For this purpose, NTC focuses to build, operate and maintain telecommunication systems throughout Pakistan to fulfill the telecom needs of Government departments.

NTC had to tread a challenging path as It started its operations with only five old vintage TDM based exchanges and analogue systems including obsolete Microwave and Co-axial Cable network/system. With these meager resources, it was extremely difficult to cope with the burgeoning Telephony and Broadband demands and provisioning of state of the art Information and Communications Technology (ICT) services to its subscribers. However, NTC overcoming the lack resources and with shear commitment of its dedicated team has pulled itself out of that feeble state and has been successful in providing secure and reliable ICT services to public sector organizations.

NTC embarked on an ambitious journey to become the leading player in the telecom industry. It started to concentrate on developing its own infrastructure. Within few years, it aligned itself with emerging technological advancements by deploying Fiber Optic Systems in the long haul (4,300 Km) and metropolitan areas (1,395 Km) along with around 383,798 digital lines exchanges.

First cloud based ISO certified Tier-III National Data Center was also established by NTC in record period of five months. It is helping the Government in e-governance, e-health and e-commerce. In order to keep itself abreast with technological advancements, NTC prepared a comprehensive plan to migrate its legacy TDM exchanges with latest IP based Next Generation Network (NGN) technology. Through this migration project, approx. 313,543 Telephone and 37,500 Broadband ports have been developed and 78% subscribers have been migrated to NGN Network.

NTC signed agreements with CMOs, PTCL, DHA, Nayatel, Multinet, Supernet, VMware etc. under Public Private Partnership.

NTC has proved its worth as national flagbearer in the field of ICT by accepting the challenges and fulfilling its responsibilities in national projects like managing, registering and administrating domain name for Urdu and local languages, establishment of operations of ccTLD for Safe City Islamabad, Pakistan Educational Research Network (PERN), positioning of PAKSAT to secure and occupy the allocated Space Slot (2003) and induction of GPON Technology and implementation of MPLS.
